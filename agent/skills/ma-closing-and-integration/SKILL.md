# Closing & Integration — Deal chỉ mới bắt đầu

Bạn là **Closing + Integration Partner BSN**. Nhiều advisor exit khi signing — BSN stay through **closing + first 100 days** để value realization thực tế.

## Phần 1: CLOSING (Signing → Closing)

### Timeline sau signing

Sell-side typically 4-16 weeks giữa signing và closing:
- Regulatory approvals (VCA 30-90 days, sector 60-120)
- CP satisfaction (third-party consents, financing)
- Interim covenants compliance
- Working capital true-up preparation

### Signing day / Closing day mechanics

**Signing day (T-day):**
- Physical or virtual signing ceremony
- All parties + advisors
- Simultaneous exchange of executed docs
- Announcement release (embargoed until confirmed)
- Champagne moment optional

**Closing day (typically T+45 to T+120):**
- Funds flow triggered (buyer to seller, per allocation)
- Share transfer executed
- Board reconstitution (new directors)
- Officer changes (bank signatory, tax authority)
- Immediate operational handover

### Post-closing regulatory (VN)

Within 10 working days:
- Amend business registration (Sở KHĐT)
- Update shareholder register
- Notify tax authority
- Notify social insurance (if change of legal entity)
- Notify sector regulators
- Update all licenses to reflect new ownership

## Phần 2: POST-MERGER INTEGRATION

### Day 1 Plan — Non-negotiables

**Communication:**
- CEO all-hands (in-person + video)
- Managers cascade briefing pre-scheduled
- Customer letter distributed
- Supplier notification sent
- Press release live
- Website updated (about, leadership, ownership)

**Operational:**
- Payroll continuity confirmed
- Payment authorization list updated
- Bank access transitioned
- IT access for new leadership
- Facilities badge for new team

**Governance:**
- New board convened
- New committee structures
- Delegation authority matrix
- Reporting cadence agreed

**Symbolic:**
- Signage / logo (if brand changing)
- Welcome from buyer CEO
- Small celebration (VN: cà phê + bánh, not overdone)

### 100-day plan structure

**Weeks 1-2: Stabilize**
- Understand what's actually running
- Meet 100 employees individually or in small groups
- Meet top 20 customers, top 10 suppliers
- Freeze any non-critical change

**Weeks 3-6: Assess**
- Diagnostic across function
- Identify quick wins vs long-term change
- Confirm integration model (absorption / preservation / symbiosis)
- Assess retention risk per key person

**Weeks 7-12: Plan detailed**
- Function integration plans (Finance, HR, IT, Sales)
- Milestone timeline
- Risk register
- Communication cadence
- Governance structure finalized

**Weeks 13-16 (Day 100 review):**
- Metrics vs baseline
- Adjust plan
- Celebrate what works
- Escalate what doesn't

### Integration models

**1. Absorption (buyer swallows target)**
- Full integration into buyer systems
- Target brand phased out
- Best for: cost synergy plays, weak target brand
- Duration: 12-24 months
- Cultural risk: HIGH — target team feels colonized

**2. Preservation (target runs as subsidiary)**
- Minimal integration
- Target keeps brand, systems, culture
- Best for: strong brand, adjacent business, cultural distance
- Duration: 6-12 months (integration light)
- Cultural risk: LOW — but synergy realization slower

**3. Symbiosis (best of both)**
- Selective integration
- Keep target's strengths, add buyer's capability
- Best for: complementary strengths
- Duration: 18-36 months
- Cultural risk: MEDIUM — requires strong integration leadership

**4. Holding (financial only)**
- Zero operational integration
- Reporting + financial consolidation only
- Best for: PE / financial buyers
- Duration: N/A
- Cultural risk: LOW

### Function-by-function integration

**Finance (Weeks 4-16):**
- Chart of accounts alignment
- Consolidation reporting
- Working capital management transition
- Tax structure optimization
- Audit rationalization
- Treasury integration

**HR (Weeks 2-24):**
- Employee data migration to buyer HRIS
- Compensation review (harmonize but avoid unrest)
- Benefits harmonization (over 12-18 months)
- Performance management alignment
- Culture assessment + intervention
- Retention program activation

**IT (Weeks 4-52):**
- IT asset inventory
- Systems integration roadmap
- Data migration
- Security policy alignment
- Vendor consolidation
- License optimization

**Sales / Commercial (Weeks 1-12):**
- Customer retention priority
- Cross-sell opportunity mapping
- Pricing coordination (careful — anti-trust)
- Sales team territory / comp alignment
- CRM consolidation

**Operations (Weeks 1-24):**
- Supply chain review
- Vendor rationalization
- Facility optimization
- Production capacity balancing
- Quality standards alignment

**Legal / Compliance (Weeks 1-8):**
- Policy harmonization
- Contract review
- Regulatory posture
- IP consolidation

### Cultural integration — VN specific

**Key insights:**
- VN family-owned business often has **founder-centric** culture — decisions escalate to owner
- Post-deal, if founder leaves, decision paralysis common
- **Loyalty to boss** stronger than loyalty to company — key manager departure triggers team exits
- **Face** important — never publicly criticize former practice

**Cultural integration playbook:**

1. **Bridge person:** Retain founder or senior VN exec as bridge for 12-24 months
2. **Language:** Communicate in Vietnamese (at least parallel to English)
3. **Small wins visible:** Celebrate integration milestones (even small ones) — team lunches, kaizen boards
4. **Preserve identity:** Keep company name / brand at least Year 1 if it has equity
5. **Respect hierarchy:** VN business more hierarchical than Western — don't flatten too fast
6. **New year / Tet:** Traditional bonuses (13th month, Tet gift) — do NOT cut in first year
7. **Fair severance:** If layoff needed, be generous — VN networks talk

### Retention monitoring

Track monthly for top 30 people:
- Attendance patterns
- Meeting participation
- Communication engagement
- LinkedIn activity (updated profile? new "open to work"?)
- Recruiter contact reports
- 1-on-1 sentiment check

Red flags:
- Update LinkedIn to "senior manager" or vague title
- Sudden vacation
- Missing key meetings
- Skip-level meetings requested
- New certifications / degrees mentioned

Intervene early:
- CEO 1-on-1
- Compensation review
- Role expansion
- Special project
- Retention bonus enhancement

## Common closing / PMI failures

### Closing failures
1. **Missed CP** — one condition not met → force renegotiation or drop
2. **VCA denial** — deal killer, wasted 3-6 months
3. **Financing fall-through** — bank changes terms last minute
4. **WC dispute** — parties disagree on final number, litigation
5. **Interim covenant breach** — seller does something forbidden (dividend, capex, hire)
6. **Signing ≠ Closing** — many deals sign but never close (~10-15%)

### PMI failures
1. **No Day 1 plan** — chaotic transition, employee panic
2. **Over-integrate too fast** — kill target's magic
3. **Under-integrate** — no synergy realized, purchase price wasted
4. **Founder exit too fast** — key relationships lost
5. **Culture clash unmanaged** — silent resistance, quality drops
6. **Key customer defection** — assumed retention, didn't proactively engage
7. **Systems migration disaster** — 6-month project becomes 24-month
8. **Synergy over-promised** — miss target by 50%, board angry
9. **Retention program too late** — top people already left
10. **No 100-day review** — integration drifts

Financial:
- Revenue vs plan
- EBITDA vs plan
- Synergy captured vs plan

Operational:
- Customer retention (top 20)

- Litigation any new

Cultural:

- Closing execution support: **80-150 triệu VND** one-off
- 100-day integration planning: **200-400 triệu VND**
- 12-month integration monitoring: **50-100 triệu/tháng**
- Post-deal dispute resolution support (if needed): hourly billing

## When PMI is done

**Rule of thumb:** integration is "done" when:
1. Financial: consolidated reporting fully automated
2. Systems: 90%+ integration complete
3. Culture: engagement scores back to baseline (took 18-36 months typically)
4. Synergy: 70%+ of Year 3 targets achieved
5. Governance: no more integration standing committee needed

Typical duration:
- Absorption: 18-30 months
- Symbiosis: 24-36 months
- Preservation: 6-12 months (light touch)
- Holding: 3-6 months (financial only)
