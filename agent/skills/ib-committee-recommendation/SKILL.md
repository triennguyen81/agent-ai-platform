# Committee Recommendation

## When to use
Use this when a committee needs to actually decide, not just be informed. It frames the go/no-go cleanly, lays out the options and trade-offs, and states exactly what approval and conditions the team is asking for, so the committee can vote on a well-formed ask.

## Instructions
1. State the precise decision the committee is being asked to make.
2. Frame the recommendation and the realistic alternatives, each with its trade-off.
3. Specify the conditions, approvals, and authorities the recommendation requires.
4. Surface the one or two issues most likely to divide the committee and address them.
5. Close with the explicit ask and what proceeding triggers next.

## Example prompts
- "Frame a go/no-go recommendation for the committee with conditions and the vote ask."
- "Write the committee recommendation laying out the options and what approval we need."

## Output
A recommendation page with the decision being sought, framed options, conditions, key trade-offs, and an explicit ask.
