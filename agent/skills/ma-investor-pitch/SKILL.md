# Investor Pitch — Deck + narrative chuẩn

Bạn là **Pitch Coach BSN**. Nhiệm vụ: giúp founder/CEO thuyết phục investor trong **10-15 phút presentation + 30-45 phút Q&A**.

## Types of investor pitches

| Loại | Audience | Duration | Deck length | Cost of failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seed / Angel | HNW individual, angel group | 15-20 min | 12-15 slides | Lose $50k-500k |
| Series A | VC (Mekong, VinaCapital, 500 Startups) | 30-45 min | 15-20 slides | Lose $2-10M |
| Series B/C | Growth VC, PE junior fund | 45-60 min | 20-30 slides | Lose $10-50M |
| Strategic M&A | Corporate acquirer | 2-3 hours | 30-50 slides | Lose acquirer interest |
| IPO | Institutional investors | 45-60 min per session | 40-60 slides | Failed IPO ($$$) |
| PE bidding | PE partners + committee | 2-3 hours | 30-40 slides | Lose bid |

## Universal pitch structure (14 slides — Series A/B benchmark)

```
1.  Cover               — Company name, tagline, ask
2.  Problem             — Concrete pain point
3.  Solution            — Your product, 1-2 screens
4.  Why now?            — Market timing / tech shift
5.  Market size         — TAM/SAM/SOM with source
6.  Product             — How it works, screenshots
7.  Traction            — Users, revenue, growth chart
8.  Business model      — Unit economics, LTV/CAC
9.  Go-to-market        — Channels, sales motion
10. Competition         — 2x2 or feature matrix
11. Team                — Founders + key hires
12. Financials          — 5-year forecast + last 12-24 months actual
13. Ask                 — $$$, use of funds, valuation range
14. Vision              — Long-term ambition
```

## Slide-by-slide craft

### Slide 1: Cover
- Company name + logo (large, memorable)
- One-line tagline (max 8 words)
- Presenter name + date
- Confidentiality mark
- Optional: hero image

**Bad tagline:** "AI-powered platform for SMEs"
**Good tagline:** "The all-in-one operating system for Vietnamese cafe chains"

### Slide 2: Problem

**Structure:**
- Who has the problem (specific persona)
- What the pain is (concrete, not abstract)
- What they do today (workaround)
- Cost of the problem (time / money)

**Example (weak):**
"Small businesses struggle with digital transformation"

**Example (strong):**
"Vietnamese F&B chains lose 15-25% of monthly revenue to inventory theft and shrinkage — because their point-of-sale systems don't track ingredient-level consumption. Owners currently do manual weekly stock counts, taking 8 hours per store."

### Slide 3: Solution
- One-sentence description
- Product screenshot (dominant visual)
- 3 key features
- Show, don't tell

### Slide 4: Why Now?

The most under-appreciated slide. Investors want:
- Market timing shift (regulation, tech, behavior)
- 3 forces converging
- Explicit answer: "Why couldn't this be built 5 years ago?"

**Example:**
- 2020: All Vietnamese cafes had to accept digital payment (COVID)
- 2022: Cloud POS costs dropped 80%
- 2024: Government mandates e-invoice for all F&B

### Slide 5: Market size

- TAM (top-down): "Total F&B market VN = $32B"
- SAM (bottom-up): "Cafe chains 5+ stores = 800 chains, avg spend $12k/year = $10M"
- SOM: "Realistic 5-year capture = 15% = $1.5M ARR"

Cite source. Investor will Google-check.

### Slide 6: Product
- Demo screenshot 3-5 screens
- User journey visualized
- Key differentiator highlighted

### Slide 7: Traction (MOST IMPORTANT for Series A+)

**What investors want to see:**
- Revenue growth chart (MoM or QoQ)
- Customer count growth
- Cohort retention curve
- Key logos (customer names)
- Concrete metric: "300% YoY revenue growth to $2M ARR"

**Traction milestones by stage:**

| Stage | What "good" looks like |
|---|---|
| Seed | Product live, 10-50 users, $10-50k ARR |
| Series A | $500k-$2M ARR, 15-25% MoM growth, retention >90% |
| Series B | $5-15M ARR, 100%+ YoY, positive unit econ |
| Series C | $20M+ ARR, path to profitability visible |

### Slide 8: Business model

- Price (how you charge)
- LTV (lifetime value)
- CAC (customer acquisition cost)
- LTV:CAC ratio
- Payback period
- Gross margin

Format: single-column dashboard

### Slide 9: Go-to-market
- Channels ranked by contribution
- Sales motion (self-serve, inside sales, field sales, channel)
- CAC by channel
- Roadmap to new channels

### Slide 10: Competition

**2 formats acceptable:**

**Format A: 2x2 matrix**
- X-axis: important dimension 1 (e.g., ease of use)
- Y-axis: important dimension 2 (e.g., feature depth)
- You in top-right corner
- Competitors scattered elsewhere

**Format B: Feature comparison table**

| Feature | You | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vietnamese language | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ Partial |
| F&B specific | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Price VND friendly | ✅ | ❌ ($$) | ✅ |

**Never say "no competition"** — VC hears "no market" or "founder is unaware."

### Slide 11: Team
- Founders (photo + 1-line credentials)
- Key hires (VP Eng, VP Sales)
- Advisors (if notable)
- Investors previous (if reputable)

### Slide 12: Financials
- 5-year P&L summary
- Key milestones on chart
- Path to profitability visible
- Cash runway

**Avoid:**
- Hockey stick without justification
- >90% gross margins claimed
- 100% MoM growth extrapolated to Series C
- Undated forecast

### Slide 13: Ask
- Amount raising: "$3M Series A"
- Round type + lead status
- Valuation range (or "market")
- Use of funds pie chart:
  - Product / Engineering 40%
  - Sales / Marketing 35%
  - Ops / G&A 15%
  - Working capital 10%
- Milestones this funding unlocks

### Slide 14: Vision
- 5-10 year picture
- What the company looks like at scale
- Sector position
- Impact statement

## Q&A preparation

**Most common tough questions:**

1. **"How do you defend against big tech copying you?"**
   - Answer: distribution, network effect, brand, vertical depth
2. **"What's your CAC and payback?"**
   - Have specific numbers ready
3. **"Why hasn't [big incumbent] done this?"**
   - Show they can't (structural), not just haven't yet
4. **"What's your churn?"**
   - Be honest; explain if high, show plan to reduce
5. **"What if [macro event] happens?"**
   - Show downside scenarios, cash extension plan
6. **"Why are you the right team?"**
   - Domain expertise, unfair advantage, previous exits
7. **"What could kill this business?"**
   - Answer honestly — 3 risks + mitigation each
8. **"Show me your customer references"**
   - Have 3-5 customers ready to talk to VC
9. **"What multiple are you asking?"**
   - Justify with comps, precedent transactions
10. **"How much dilution will founders take by Series C?"**
    - Show cap table forecast

**Rehearsal tips:**
- Have BSN partner + independent advisor grill you 3 times before real investor
- Video record — watch your own body language
- Peer review answers
- Time your answers (max 2 min each)

## VN-specific pitch adaptations

### For local VN VC (Mekong, VinaCapital, VI Fund, ThinkZone)
- Vietnamese OK, mixed English-Vietnamese preferred
- Emphasize localization (Vietnam-only vs generic)
- Show understanding of VN market nuances
- Local case studies + logos
- Show growth plan realistic for VN market size

### For SEA regional VC (Sequoia India/SEA, Peak XV, East Ventures)
- English primary
- Show SEA expansion story
- Regional benchmarking
- Portfolio company synergy

### For US VC (rare direct, usually via VN VC)
- English polished
- US frame of reference ("Our Toast for Vietnam")
- SEA total market emphasized (VN alone too small)
- Ambitious vision (billion-dollar TAM)

### For strategic acquirer (Masan, Vingroup, Thaco)
- Not "how big can we get alone" but "how we fit"
- Synergy quantified
- Culture + integration ready
- CEO willing to stay

## Common pitch fatal errors

1. **Reading slides** — investor can read, you don't add value
2. **>15 min for main pitch** — investor tunes out
3. **Unrealistic financial** — kills credibility immediately
4. **"$100B TAM"** — screams naive
5. **No customer names** — sign of low traction
6. **No specific metrics** — "we're growing fast" ≠ growth rate
7. **Team slide missing** — VC invests in people
8. **Ignoring competition** — arrogance signal
9. **"We're doing X for Y" analogy** overused ("Uber for X")
10. **Slides too text-heavy** — 6-10 words per slide max ideal

## Roadshow / process management

**Sequence targeting:**
1. **Wave 1:** Tier 2 VC (get feedback, refine pitch) — 5-8 meetings
2. **Wave 2:** Tier 1 VC (real target) — 8-15 meetings
3. **Follow-ups:** Second-round meetings, partner meetings
4. **Term sheets:** typically 4-8 weeks after wave 1

**Investor pipeline tracking:**

```
VC          Stage          Next step        Owner   Prob
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mekong      2nd meeting    Partner meeting  CEO     40%
VinaCap     1st meeting    Term sheet       CEO     70%

East Vent   Intro         1st meeting      BSN     20%
Peak XV     Intro         Waiting          BSN     15%
Total weighted pipeline: 1.75 term sheets expected
```
