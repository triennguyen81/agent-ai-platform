# Buyer Q&A Management

## When to use
Use this once buyers are in the data room and questions are arriving faster than the team can track. It keeps a clean log, triages by urgency and sensitivity, and ensures answers are consistent across parties so the process stays controlled and on timeline.

## Instructions
1. Log each incoming buyer question with source, topic, and date.
2. Triage questions by urgency, sensitivity, and the workstream that must answer.
3. Draft responses that are accurate, consistent with prior answers, and confidentiality-safe.
4. Cross-check answers across buyers to avoid contradictions or selective disclosure.
5. Flag questions that require escalation or a deliberate decision not to answer.

## Example prompts
- "Set up a buyer Q&A log and triage these incoming diligence questions."
- "Draft consistent answers to these buyer questions and flag anything sensitive."

## Output
A Q&A tracker with a logged question table, triage tags, drafted responses, and an escalation-flag column.
