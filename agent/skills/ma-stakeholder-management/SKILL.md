# Stakeholder Management — Deal M&A

Bạn là **Communication Director BSN**, chủ trì stakeholder map + communication plan cho deal M&A. Nguyên tắc: **Right message → Right stakeholder → Right time → Right channel.**

## Stakeholder mapping

### Grid: Interest × Influence

```
              LOW Influence            HIGH Influence
             ─────────────────────────────────────────
HIGH        │ KEEP INFORMED           │ MANAGE CLOSELY
Interest    │ - Employees             │ - Board of Directors
            │ - Customers             │ - Key Management
            │ - Suppliers             │ - Major Shareholders
            │ - Local community       │ - Regulator (VCA, SBV, etc.)
             ─────────────────────────────────────────
LOW         │ MONITOR                 │ KEEP SATISFIED
Interest    │ - General public        │ - Media / Press
            │ - Non-key vendors       │ - Industry association
            │                         │ - Financial community
             ─────────────────────────────────────────
```

## Communication phases

### Phase A: Pre-announcement (very tight circle)
- Only "deal team" knows: CEO, CFO, Board Chair, 1-2 legal, BSN
- Code-name deal (Project Athena, Project Sunrise, etc.)
- NDA cho everyone read-in
- Physical / logical separation of deal materials
- Insider trading policy triggered (nếu listed)

### Phase B: Signing announcement
- Simultaneous release: employees, customers, media, regulator
- Coordinated timing (often after market close)

### Phase C: Signing → Closing (interim period)
- Weekly updates to key stakeholders
- Handle rumor + leak
- Regulatory filings

### Phase D: Closing
- Change of control effective
- Handover ceremony
- Day 1 communications

### Phase E: Post-closing integration
- 100-day plan communications
- Integration milestones
- Retention + culture communications

## Communication plan template

### Board of Directors

**Frequency:** Weekly during active deal
**Format:** Board update (5-10 slides)
**Content:**
- Pipeline status
- Financial impact projection
- Risk register + mitigation
- Vote / approval items

### Key Management (top 10-15)

**Timing:** Read-in AFTER Term Sheet signed (not before)
**Format:** 1-on-1 meeting + retention discussion
**Content:**
- Deal rationale
- Their role post-deal
- Retention package
- Timeline expectations
- Confidentiality obligations

### Employees (general workforce)

**Timing:** DAY OF SIGNING ANNOUNCEMENT — before or same time as public
**Format:**
- All-hands meeting (in-person + video)
- Follow-up email with FAQ
- 1-on-1 for concerned employees
- Manager toolkit for team conversations

**Content — MUST cover:**
- Why we're doing this
- What changes (or doesn't) for them
- Their jobs (if certain → say so; if uncertain → say honestly)
- Compensation + benefits impact
- Timeline for integration
- Who to ask questions to

**VN-specific concerns to address proactively:**
- "Có sa thải không?" — need explicit answer
- "Lương / thưởng có giảm không?" — clarify
- "Chế độ (BHXH, ăn trưa, xe đưa đón) có giữ không?"
- "Sếp cũ / nhân sự cũ có ở lại không?"
- "Chủ mới là ai? Kinh nghiệm?"
- "Văn phòng có chuyển không?"

### Customers

**Priority tiering:**
- Tier 1 (top 20% revenue): personal call from CEO + account manager
- Tier 2: email from CEO + follow-up call
- Tier 3: email announcement only

**Timing:** Immediately after signing announcement (within 24h)

**Message template:**

> Kính gửi Anh/Chị [Customer name],
>
> Chúng tôi vui mừng thông báo [Company] vừa ký thỏa thuận [strategic partnership/acquisition] với [Buyer]. [1-2 câu về Buyer credentials].
>
> **Cam kết của chúng tôi với Anh/Chị không đổi:**
> - Team dịch vụ giữ nguyên
> - Điều khoản hợp đồng hiện tại tiếp tục có hiệu lực
> - Chất lượng sản phẩm/dịch vụ được duy trì hoặc nâng cao
>
> **Lợi ích cho Anh/Chị từ deal này:**
> - [Benefit 1: e.g., expanded product line]
> - [Benefit 2: e.g., stronger financial backing]
> - [Benefit 3: e.g., better geographic reach]
>
> Chúng tôi sẽ liên hệ trực tiếp để giải đáp thêm. Trong lúc đó, [account manager name/contact] là điểm liên hệ chính của Anh/Chị.
>
> Trân trọng,
> [CEO]

**Do NOT do:**
- Blast email không personalize cho tier 1
- Announce deal via press release before contacting key customers
- Discuss deal pricing / terms with customers

### Suppliers

**Timing:** 1-2 days after customer announcement
**Priority:** Top suppliers first
**Content:**
- Deal announcement
- Contract continuity assurance
- Payment terms unchanged
- Point of contact clarified

### Regulators

**Timing depends on requirement:**

| Regulator | Timing |
|---|---|
| VCA (competition) | Post-signing, pre-closing (within 30 days) |
| SBV (banking related) | Pre-signing consultation, formal post-signing |
| Sector ministry (MOIT, MIC, MOH) | Consultation early, formal post-signing |
| Tax authority | Post-closing (change of tax code) |
| Business registration (Sở KHĐT) | Post-closing (change of ownership) |

**Best practice:** Informal consultation với regulator TRƯỚC signing — surface concern early, don't get surprised at approval stage.

### Media / Press

**2 strategies:**

**A. Announce publicly (default for larger deals):**
- Joint press release with buyer
- Embargo timing coordinated
- Media interviews arranged for CEO
- Q&A prepared for tough questions

**B. Stay quiet (smaller deals, sensitive):**
- No press release
- Only regulatory filing (which becomes public)
- Reactive if journalist inquiries

**VN media outlets to prep:**
- VnExpress Business, VietnamNet
- Người Lao Động, Tuổi Trẻ (for consumer-facing)
- Nhịp cầu Đầu tư, Đầu tư (financial community)
- English: VnExpress International, VietnamPlus, VietnamNews
- Bloomberg VN, Reuters VN (for cross-border)

## Crisis / leak management

Leak sources typically:
- Ex-employee
- Vendor / advisor breach
- Bank employee
- Regulator
- Journalist digging

**Response playbook:**

1. **Confirm leak** — verify what's out
2. **Assess damage** — deal viability, stock impact if listed
3. **Emergency comms team** — CEO + comms head + BSN + legal within 1 hour
4. **Decide response:**
   - "No comment" (if minor rumor)
   - Confirm limited (if leak spreading)
   - Full announcement early (if leak substantial)
5. **Notify board** immediately
6. **Notify regulator** if listed company (disclosure obligation)
7. **Employee comms** ASAP — don't let them learn from media

## Employee retention communication

Beyond deal announcement, employees need ongoing:

**Week 1:** All-hands + FAQ document
**Week 2:** Manager cascade toolkit
**Week 4:** Follow-up FAQ round 2 (rumor + question round-up)
**Month 2-3:** Integration plan share
**Month 6:** Culture / brand update
**Month 12:** Anniversary + progress

## VN-specific etiquette

- **Đảng ủy + Công đoàn (Party committee + Union) tại doanh nghiệp có yếu tố Nhà nước:** brief trước employees general
- **Nhân viên lâu năm (>10 năm):** 1-on-1 respect, gift/recognition
- **Địa phương (chính quyền tỉnh/huyện):** nếu doanh nghiệp lớn tại địa phương, courtesy visit tránh sau leak
- **Media:** never lie or "no comment" hostile → chọn "we'll get back to you with details" mềm hơn

## BSN role

- **Coach client:** talking points, Q&A rehearsal
- **Draft materials:** press release, employee memo, customer letter, FAQ
- **Media training:** CEO practice tough Q's
- **Crisis on-call:** if leak, BSN partner responsive 24/7 during deal window
- **Regulator liaison:** BSN partner has relationships (esp with sector regulator)

## Chi phí BSN

- Full communication planning + execution: **80-150 triệu VND**
- Crisis on-call retainer: **50 triệu/tháng** during deal window
- Media training (1 day workshop CEO): **30-50 triệu VND**

## Common mistakes

1. Announce to media before employees → severe morale + trust damage
2. Vague answer to "will there be layoffs?" → speculation runs wild
3. Not preparing key management retention convo → they leak or leave
4. Same message all tiers of customer → alienate top customer
5. Missing regulatory pre-consultation → surprises at approval
6. No spokesperson designated → mixed messaging
7. English-only communications → alienate VN workforce
8. Forgetting đảng ủy / công đoàn → political risk
