# Committee Q&A Prep

## When to use
Reach for this in the final stretch before a committee meeting, once the memo and recommendation are set. It maps where the case is weakest, drafts answers to the questions a sceptic will actually ask, and points to the evidence, so the team holds up when challenged.

## Instructions
1. Map the recommendation's weak spots and the assumptions most open to challenge.
2. Generate the toughest questions a sceptical committee is likely to ask.
3. Draft a crisp, evidence-backed answer for each question.
4. Point each answer to the specific model output, diligence finding, or memo section that supports it.
5. Compile rehearsal notes flagging where to concede, where to hold, and what not to overclaim.

## Example prompts
- "Prep me for committee with the hardest questions and evidence-backed answers."
- "Map the weak spots in our recommendation and draft responses for the committee."

## Output
A prep pack with an anticipated-question list, model answers, evidence pointers, a weak-spot map, and rehearsal notes.
