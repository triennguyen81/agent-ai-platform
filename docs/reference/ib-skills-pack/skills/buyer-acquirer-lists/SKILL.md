# Buyer Acquirer Lists

## When to use
Use this after a target or asset is identified and you need to define who the realistic buyers or acquirers are, segmented by strategic logic and ability to pay. It converts a vague "who might want this" into a tiered, rationale-backed outreach universe ready for process planning.

## Instructions
1. Restate the asset profile and the strategic or financial logic a counterparty would be buying into.
2. Generate candidate strategics (adjacent capabilities, geography, vertical integration) and financial sponsors (mandate fit, fund size, prior deals).
3. Assess each candidate's strategic fit and likely ability to pay, noting synergy angles or portfolio overlap.
4. Tier the universe into priority, secondary, and opportunistic outreach groups.
5. Attach a tailored angle and likely objection for each priority counterparty.

## Example prompts
- "Build a tiered buyer list for a 50M-revenue industrial software asset, strategics and sponsors."
- "Who are the realistic acquirers for this regional logistics business and why?"

## Output
A segmented buyer-universe table (strategic/financial, tier, fit rationale, angle) plus a short prioritization note.
