# Sector Screen Mapping

## When to use
Reach for this when entering a new vertical or refreshing coverage and you need a structured pass over the landscape rather than a gut-feel list. It is the first move before any buyer list or thesis, turning a broad sector into a defensible, prioritized field of names.

## Instructions
1. Define the sector boundary and the screening criteria (size, growth, margin, ownership, fragmentation) tied to the strategic question.
2. Assemble a candidate universe from public filings, sector databases, and trade sources, recording each company's key metrics.
3. Score and rank candidates against the weighted criteria, flagging data gaps and outliers for manual review.
4. Cluster the ranked names into tiers (priority, watch, exclude) with a one-line rationale per company.
5. Summarize the thematic drivers and the three to five names that most warrant deeper origination work.

## Example prompts
- "Screen the regional waste-management sector for fragmented, founder-owned targets above 10M EBITDA."
- "Build me a ranked shortlist of specialty-chemicals companies worth pitching this quarter."

## Output
A markdown brief with a screening-criteria table, a tiered company ranking table, and a short thematic-drivers narrative.
