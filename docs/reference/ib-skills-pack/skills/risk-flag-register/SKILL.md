# Risk Flag Register

## When to use
Use this to pull scattered diligence findings into one prioritized view of risk. It scores and ranks issues by how badly they could hurt the deal and proposes mitigants, giving the committee a clear-eyed list of what could move price or kill the transaction.

## Instructions
1. Gather candidate risks from every diligence workstream into a single list.
2. Score each risk on severity and likelihood with a brief evidence note.
3. Translate each risk into its concrete deal impact (price, structure, conditions, walk-away).
4. Propose a mitigant or diligence step for each material risk.
5. Rank the register and mark the items requiring escalation to the committee.

## Example prompts
- "Consolidate these diligence findings into a ranked risk register."
- "Score the deal risks by severity and tell me which ones to escalate."

## Output
A risk register table (risk, severity, likelihood, deal impact, mitigant, owner) with a ranked escalation list.
