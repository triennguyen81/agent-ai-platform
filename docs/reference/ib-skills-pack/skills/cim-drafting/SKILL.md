# CIM Drafting

## When to use
Reach for this once diligence inputs and financials are ready and the asset must be presented to buyers. It builds the central marketing document that frames the equity story, anchors the process, and seeds buyers' own valuation work.

## Instructions
1. Build the CIM outline covering executive summary, company, market, financials, and growth.
2. Draft the company and market sections into a coherent positioning narrative.
3. Translate the financial schedules into a clear, buyer-facing financial story.
4. Articulate the growth opportunities and equity story that justify buyer interest.
5. Review for consistency, confidentiality, and the deliberate absence of a price expectation.

## Example prompts
- "Draft a CIM for this industrial-services business from these diligence inputs."
- "Turn the company overview and financials into a buyer-ready equity story."

## Output
A CIM draft with executive summary, company overview, market section, financial narrative, and growth-opportunity sections.
