# Earnings Quality Review

## When to use
Reach for this whenever the valuation rests on an earnings figure that the counterparty has adjusted. It scrutinizes add-backs, one-offs, and run-rate claims so the team values the business on earnings that will actually persist, not a flattered number.

## Instructions
1. Reconcile reported earnings to an adjusted-EBITDA bridge and list every add-back.
2. Assess each add-back for legitimacy, recurrence, and supporting evidence.
3. Normalize earnings to a defensible run-rate, adjusting for one-offs and timing.
4. Test cash conversion against earnings to detect accruals-driven inflation.
5. Summarize the quality flags and the earnings figure the team should underwrite.

## Example prompts
- "Review earnings quality on this target and stress-test the EBITDA add-backs."
- "Build an adjusted-EBITDA bridge and tell me which add-backs won't survive."

## Output
An earnings-quality memo with an adjusted-EBITDA bridge, an add-back assessment table, a cash-conversion check, and quality flags.
