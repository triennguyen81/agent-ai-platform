# Management Presentation

## When to use
Use this when the process moves from documents to live management sessions and the team must present and defend the story in person. It shapes the deck flow, sharpens the key messages, and anticipates the hard questions so management lands the meeting.

## Instructions
1. Define the narrative arc and the three to five messages the meeting must land.
2. Structure the deck flow so each section advances the equity story.
3. Build the key-message slides with the proof points that support them.
4. Anticipate the toughest buyer questions and prepare crisp responses.
5. Add speaker guidance flagging where management should pause, emphasize, or defer.

## Example prompts
- "Build a management presentation deck from our CIM for the buyer meetings."
- "Prep the management team with anticipated buyer questions and proof points."

## Output
A presentation outline with a slide-by-slide flow, key-message slides, a proof-point list, and an anticipated-Q&A section.
