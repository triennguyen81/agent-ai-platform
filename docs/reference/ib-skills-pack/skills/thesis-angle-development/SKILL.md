# Thesis Angle Development

## When to use
Reach for this once you have a target and a buyer universe and need to articulate why the deal is compelling and timely. It forces the loose strands of research into a single defensible thesis with supporting evidence and a clear "why now," which is what a pitch actually sells.

## Instructions
1. Synthesize screening and buyer-list findings into a one-sentence working thesis.
2. Build out two to four value-creation angles (growth, consolidation, multiple arbitrage, operational lift) with supporting evidence.
3. Stress-test the thesis against the strongest counterargument and document the rebuttal.
4. Articulate the "why now" catalyst that makes the timing credible.
5. Distil the thesis into a headline plus three proof points suitable for a pitch opener.

## Example prompts
- "Turn this sector screen into a consolidation thesis I can pitch to a sponsor."
- "Develop the why-now angle for acquiring this founder-owned manufacturer."

## Output
A one-page thesis brief with a headline statement, value-creation angles, evidence bullets, key risks, and a why-now paragraph.
